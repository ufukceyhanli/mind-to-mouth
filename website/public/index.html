Why talking out loud works

Clear speech is a habit, not a talent. The drill is small enough to keep.

Thinking and speaking at once

Most of us can write a tidy paragraph. Saying one, live, with no backspace key, is a different muscle. This trains it.

A random word removes the excuse

You cannot prepare for "lantern". You learn to find an angle fast and commit to it, which is what meetings and interviews ask of you.

Numbers you can move

Words per minute, fillers per hundred words, longest pause. Each one is measured, not guessed, so you can watch it change over weeks.

Two minutes fits any day

Long enough to run out of easy things to say, short enough to do before coffee. A streak counter keeps you honest.

Built to stay out of your way

Your talks stay on your phone

Recordings, transcripts and scores are stored on the device. Delete one talk, or everything, whenever you want.

Sent only with your say-so

To score a talk, the audio goes to an AI model service for transcription and coaching. The app asks first and you can withdraw at any time.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no device identifiers. An account holds nothing but your email address, and you can delete it in the app.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

Got questions?

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with Apple, Google or an email address. The account only identifies you; your talks never leave the phone. You can delete it from Profile → Account at any time.

What happens to my recording?

It is saved on your phone. To score the talk, the audio is sent to an AI model service that turns it into text and writes the coaching notes. The app explains this and asks before the first recording, and you can withdraw under Profile → About.

Is it free?

The app has no subscription or in-app purchases. Scoring uses free models and needs a network connection.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. An Android build exists but is not the focus; long talks there hit upload limits.

The coach said something odd. What now?

Feedback is AI-generated and labelled as such. Use the report link under the coach card, or the support page, and tell us what it said.

More on the support page: deleting talks, withdrawing consent, deleting your account.
