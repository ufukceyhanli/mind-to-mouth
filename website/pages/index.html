A daily speaking drill that teaches you to think out loud. Talk about a random word, then hear how it went.

Get the app How it works

A talk a day

  1. You get a word. Something ordinary, an idea, a situation, now and then an oddball. Reroll if you like.
  2. A few seconds to think. A short countdown, then the microphone opens.
  3. Talk for two minutes. No notes, no stopping. The clock counts down, then counts up.
  4. See the score. Pace, fluency, fillers, vocabulary and structure, with coaching notes on what worked and what to try tomorrow. Listen back any time.

Built to stay out of your way

Your talks stay on your phone

Recordings, transcripts and scores are stored on the device. Delete one talk, or everything, whenever you want.

Sent only with your say-so

To score a talk, the audio goes to an AI model service for transcription and coaching. The app asks first and you can withdraw at any time.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no device identifiers. An account holds nothing but your email address, and you can delete it in the app.

The full details are in the privacy policy. Questions and problems go to support.
