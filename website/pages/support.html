Support

Mind to Mouth is made by one developer. The quickest way to get help, report a bug, or say what the AI coach got wrong is to open an issue on GitHub. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

What does the app do with my recording?

The recording is sent to NVIDIA’s AI model service to be turned into text, and the text is sent again so an AI coach can write feedback. Nothing is sent until you agree on the notice shown before your first talk. The recording, transcript and scores are then kept on your phone, not on any server. The privacy policy has the full picture.

How do I stop sending recordings?

Tap your avatar on the Today tab to open Profile, then under About tap Withdraw next to “Sending recordings”. The app will ask again before your next talk and will not record until you agree. Talks cannot be scored without it.

How do I delete a talk?

Swipe left on it in Talks, or long-press it, or open the talk and choose Delete talk from the menu in the top-right corner. To remove everything at once, open Profile → About → Delete all talks and recordings. Deleting the app also removes every talk stored on the phone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile (your avatar on the Today tab), then under Account tap Delete account and confirm. The account and everything the sign-in service holds about it are removed immediately. Talks on the phone are not affected; delete them separately if you want them gone too.

If you can no longer open the app, open an issue titled “Delete my account”. Do not put your email address in the public issue; the developer will reply with a private way to confirm which account is yours, and will delete it once confirmed.

The coach said something wrong or unfair

The feedback is generated by an AI model and can be wrong. Use the Report a problem link under the coach card on the talk’s screen, or open an issue and paste the feedback (leave out anything personal from the transcript).

The microphone does not work

The app needs microphone access and asks for it before the first recording. If you declined, open the iPhone Settings app, scroll to Mind to Mouth and turn on Microphone.

Scoring fails or never finishes

Scoring needs a network connection, because transcription and coaching run on the model service. Check the connection and try again from the talk’s screen. If it keeps failing, open an issue with the message shown.

“Continue with Google” says it is not set up

Google sign-in is still being switched on. Use Continue with Apple or create an account with your email in the meantime.

Contact

Everything goes through the project’s GitHub issues: github.com/ufukceyhanli/mind-to-mouth/issues. You need a free GitHub account to open one. The app’s source code is public in the same place, so you can see for yourself what it does with your data.
