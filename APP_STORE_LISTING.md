**Description (4000)**

```
Mind to Mouth is a daily drill for thinking out loud.

You get one random word. You have a few seconds to think. Then you talk about it for two minutes, with no notes and no script. When you finish, the app shows how it went and what to try tomorrow.

It is built for anyone who writes well but freezes when asked to speak: in meetings, in interviews, on calls, in a second language.

HOW IT WORKS
• A word appears. Something ordinary, an idea, a situation, now and then an oddball.
• A short countdown gives you time to find an angle.
• You talk for two minutes. The clock counts down to the target, then keeps counting up.
• Your talk is transcribed and scored, and a coach writes notes on it.

WHAT YOU SEE AFTER EACH TALK
• An overall score out of 100
• Pace in words per minute, and how much it varied
• Filler words: um, uh, like, you know
• Vocabulary range and how well you stayed on the word
• Your longest pause and your speaking volume
• Two or three strengths, what to work on, and one drill for tomorrow
• The transcript and the recording, to listen back

WHY IT WORKS
You cannot prepare for a random word. You learn to pick an angle fast and commit to it, which is exactly what real conversations ask of you. Two minutes is long enough to run out of easy things to say and short enough to do every day. A streak keeps you honest.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Recordings, transcripts and scores are stored on your iPhone
• Speech to text runs on your iPhone; only the transcript goes to the coach, and only after you agree
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Delete one talk, all talks, or your account from inside the app

FREE AND PLUS
The free plan includes three scored talks a week, each with the full score and coaching notes.

Mind to Mouth Plus is an optional monthly subscription that adds:
• Unlimited talks, so you can practise every day
• A pronunciation check that plays back the words worth a second try
• Talks up to ten minutes

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically every month unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Feedback from the coach is generated by AI and can be wrong. It is practice guidance, not professional advice.

Privacy policy: https://mind-to-mouth.web.app/privacy
Terms of use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
```

## Age rating questionnaire

Answer **None** or **No** to every content category (violence, sexual content,
profanity, drugs, gambling, horror, medical information, contests). The app has
no web browsing, no user-to-user messaging and no content shared between users.
Where it asks about AI: the app shows AI-generated feedback to the user who
recorded the talk, and to nobody else. Expected rating: 4+.
